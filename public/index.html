THE BEAUTIFUL GAME

How to play Tak

Play with a friend on the same device. Ivory goes first. Connect the left and right edges, or the top and bottom edges, with a road of your visible flat stones and capstones. Connections are orthogonal, never diagonal.

1. The opening swap

On each of the first two turns, place one opponent’s flat stone on an empty square. After that, play your own pieces.

2. Place or move

On your turn, place a flat, standing stone (wall), or capstone on an empty square, or move a stack with your color on top. Flats and standing stones share the same reserve.

3. Spread a stack

Carry any number of top pieces up to the board width. Travel in one straight direction, dropping at least one piece per square from the bottom of the carried stack. Select Move a stack, click your stack, choose a carry count and drop pattern, then click a highlighted destination. For example, carrying 3 and dropping 1,2 travels two squares.

4. Walls and capstones

You can stack on flats, but never on capstones. A standing stone blocks movement unless the final drop is a single capstone with nothing beneath it in the carried stack. That capstone flattens the wall. Walls are not part of roads.

5. Winning

A road wins immediately. If a move creates roads for both players, the mover wins. If the board fills or either player uses all their stones and capstones, count only visible flat stones (not walls or capstones). The higher count wins; equal counts are a draw. A road takes priority over flat scoring.
